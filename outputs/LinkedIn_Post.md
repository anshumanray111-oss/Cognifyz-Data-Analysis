## Post Copy

🎉 Excited to announce the completion of my **Data Analysis Internship** at **Cognifyz IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd.**! 🚀

During this internship, I worked with a comprehensive multi-country restaurant dataset to perform end-to-end exploratory data analysis, data cleaning, statistical modeling, and data visualization using **Python** and the data science ecosystem.

Per the Cognifyz program guidelines, I chose **Level 1 + Level 2** to build an in-depth analytical project covering 8 key tasks:

📊 **Level 1 — Market Characterization:**
1️⃣ **Top Cuisines:** Handled comma-separated multi-cuisine data by unnesting and calculating true individual cuisine frequency and platform coverage.
2️⃣ **City Analysis:** Identified top restaurant hubs, evaluated average ratings per city, and accounted for sample-size limitations when interpreting city-level performance.
3️⃣ **Price Range Distribution:** Visualized restaurant distribution across price tiers, highlighting the dominance of budget-to-mid-market dining.
4️⃣ **Online Delivery:** Analyzed delivery adoption and compared ratings between delivery-enabled and non-delivery establishments (framing associations responsibly without claiming causation).

📈 **Level 2 — Deep-Dive & Relationship Analysis:**
5️⃣ **Restaurant Ratings:** Analyzed rating distributions, identified the primary rating cluster (3.5–4.5), and evaluated vote skewness.
6️⃣ **Cuisine Combinations:** Canonicalized multi-cuisine pairings to analyze recurring combinations and their associated ratings with sample size controls.
7️⃣ **Geographic Analysis:** Filtered invalid coordinates and mapped restaurant spatial distribution to identify geographic clusters.
8️⃣ **Restaurant Chains:** Identified multi-location brands and benchmarked chain reach, total engagement (votes), and average customer satisfaction.

💡 **Key Takeaways & Business Thinking:**
Beyond writing clean code, this project sharpened my ability to translate data observations into practical business implications — looking at how menu composition, geographic concentration, and pricing dynamics shape food aggregator strategies.

🛠️ **Tech Stack:** Python | pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Folium | Jupyter Notebook

A big thank you to the team at **Cognifyz IT Solutions** for this practical learning opportunity!

Looking forward to applying these data analysis and analytical problem-solving skills to future challenges. 💼
